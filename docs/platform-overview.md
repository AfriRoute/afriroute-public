# Platform Overview

AfriRoute is a communications, automation, and AI platform that helps organizations
engage their customers securely and reliably across multiple channels.

## What AfriRoute offers

- **Messaging** — reach customers over common messaging channels for notifications,
  reminders, and conversations.
- **Voice** — programmable voice for alerts, confirmations, and customer support.
- **Verification** — confirm user identity and contact details to reduce fraud and
  improve trust.
- **Payments** — support customer payment and collection journeys.
- **Automation & AI** — assistive automation and AI-supported workflows that operate
  within clear, auditable controls and human oversight.
- **Workflow** — coordinate multi-step customer journeys across the channels above.

## Principles

- **Security first.** Customer engagement is treated as sensitive activity.
- **Consent and compliance.** Communications are expected to respect applicable laws,
  recipient consent, and do-not-contact preferences.
- **Human oversight of AI.** Automated and AI-assisted actions operate within defined
  policies and remain reviewable.
