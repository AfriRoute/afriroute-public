# Acceptable Use

This acceptable-use summary applies to anyone using AfriRoute products and services.
It is a public overview and may be supplemented by the terms of a specific agreement.

## You must

- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
- Send communications only to recipients who have a lawful basis to be contacted.
- Honor opt-out, do-not-contact, and consent-withdrawal requests promptly.
- Keep accurate sender identity and avoid misleading recipients.
- Protect any personal data you process and limit it to legitimate purposes.

## You must not

- Send spam, unsolicited bulk messages, or content the recipient has opted out of.
- Use the platform for fraud, phishing, scams, or impersonation.
- Distribute malware, harmful code, or deceptive links.
- Send unlawful, abusive, hateful, or harassing content.
- Attempt to bypass security controls, rate limits, or compliance safeguards.
- Interfere with the integrity, performance, or availability of the platform.

## Enforcement

AfriRoute may investigate suspected violations and may suspend or terminate access to
protect recipients, the platform, and other users.

## Reporting

To report abuse or a security concern, see [Security](../SECURITY.md) and
[Contact](../CONTACT.md).
