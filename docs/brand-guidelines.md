# Brand Guidelines

The AfriRoute name, logos, and brand assets are protected. These public guidelines
describe acceptable reference use. They do not grant a license to the brand.

## Permitted reference use

- Referring to AfriRoute by name in plain text to describe an integration or
  relationship that genuinely exists.
- Using the correct spelling and capitalization: **AfriRoute** (one word, capital "A"
  and capital "R").

## Not permitted without written approval

- Using AfriRoute logos, wordmarks, or brand assets.
- Using AfriRoute names or marks in your product name, company name, domain, or logo.
- Implying partnership, sponsorship, endorsement, or certification that does not exist.
- Modifying, recoloring, or recreating AfriRoute brand assets.

## Correct usage

- Write the name as "AfriRoute," not "Afri Route," "afriroute," or "AFRIROUTE."
- Refer to products by their full public names where applicable.
